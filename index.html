Handmade Pottery, Made Right Here

Blue Heron Pottery Studio is a working ceramics studio in downtown Fernville, where every mug, bowl, and vase is thrown, glazed, and fired on site. We've been teaching wheel-throwing and hand-building classes to total beginners since 2015, no experience required for any class we offer.

New six-week beginner sessions start the first Monday of every month. Walk-ins are welcome to browse finished pieces in the studio any Tuesday through Saturday, 10am to 5pm.
